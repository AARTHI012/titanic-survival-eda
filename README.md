## 📋 Project Objective

To analyze the Titanic passenger dataset and uncover key patterns and factors that influenced the survival of passengers.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- **Source**: Titanic Dataset (Kaggle / Data Science Dojo)
- **Rows**: 891
- **Columns**: 12
- **Target Variable**: `Survived` (0 = No, 1 = Yes)

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Gender** was the strongest factor: **74%** of females survived vs **19%** of males.
- **Passenger Class** had a major impact: 1st Class had ~63% survival rate, while 3rd Class had only ~24%.
- **Children** had higher survival rates ("Women and Children First").
- Passengers who paid **higher fares** had better chances of survival.
- Traveling with a small family increased survival chances.
- Embarkation port also showed some correlation with survival.
